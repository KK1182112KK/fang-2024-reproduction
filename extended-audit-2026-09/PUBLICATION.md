## Committed versus regenerated

Committed: executable sampled-data audit, independent continuous-feedback/spectrum audit, tests, optional PDF-vector extractor, text report, compact spectrum/refinement/small-state/figure metrics, local test record and provenance.

Regenerated by the scripts: full 18-case sampled trajectories, all continuous-feedback trajectories, transport arrays/plots, and comparison figures. These large CSV/NPZ/PNG outputs remain in the separately supplied audit archive rather than being duplicated in Git history.

The archived audit reported 17 local tests passed. Any GitHub CI result is a separate execution of the committed files. No MATLAB execution, author-code access, interval-certified full-spectrum computation, certified Theorem-1 epsilon, or misconduct claim is made.
